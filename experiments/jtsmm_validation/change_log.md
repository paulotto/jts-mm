# Change log of the analysis plan (system validation study)

The analysis plan of the system validation study (two participants, P1 and P2) is
[`analysis_plan.md`](analysis_plan.md). It was frozen on 2026-10-22, before any agreement
result was looked at, and its text is unchanged since. This file lists every change that was
logged after the freeze, one row per entry (71 rows), with its date, its reason, and whether
results had been seen. The entries keep their ids (VD for deviations and added analyses, EV
for errata), so that the references in the paper and in the code can be found.

**Results seen.** The column repeats what the working log states: "no" means the entry was
defined before the values it governs were computed, "yes" means it was made after its own or
related results were known, "n/a" marks a correction of wording or documentation without a
value change, and "not stated" means the log makes no statement. Values quoted in a row are
those of the time of the entry. The values of the paper come from the final frozen snapshot
and can differ.

Abbreviations: CI, confidence interval. CCC, concordance correlation coefficient. CMC, coefficient of multiple correlation. MVC, maximum voluntary contraction. SE, standard error. "Calibration tool" is the tracked pointer that marks the anatomical landmarks. "Own frames" are the anatomical frames that each system builds from its own landmark marking (the frozen primary outcome).
