# Change log of the analysis plan (data-set study)

The analysis plan of the data-set study is [`analysis_plan.md`](analysis_plan.md). It was
frozen as version 1.0 on 2026-10-15, before any outcome was computed, and its text is unchanged
since. This file lists every change made after the freeze, one row per entry, with its date,
its reason, and whether results had been seen. The entries keep their ids, so that the
references in the papers and in the code can be found:

| Id | Kind | Rows |
|---|---|---|
| A | Amendment, made before the first outcome stage ran | 4 |
| E | Erratum: an error in the code or in the wording that was found and corrected | 42 |
| D, X, B | Deviation from the plan (D), added analysis (X), bench measurement (B) | 70 |
| J | Entry of the work plan of November 2026, written when the results of both papers existed | 65 |

**How to read it.** Most rows are corrections of errors and added sensitivity analyses.
Section 1 lists the changes that bear on the four pre-specified primary outcomes, with their
direction. The tables in Sections 2 to 5 are the full record, and Section 6 gives the evidence level of
every requirement row.

**Results seen.** The column reads "no" when the entry was written before the values it
governs were computed, "yes" when its own or related results were known (post hoc), "n/a" for
a correction of wording, documentation, or presentation without a value change, and "not
stated" when the working log does not say. Where an entry was added after related results
were known but its definition was fixed before its own values were computed, the cell reads
"yes, own values not yet computed". Values quoted in a row are those of the time of the entry.
The values of the papers come from the final frozen snapshot and can differ.

**Participants.** The plan was frozen when twelve adults had been recorded, and it provided
for one more (Section 2 of the plan). D17 added this participant and a participant recorded
outside the protocol. D35 and D36 fixed the reporting population of the papers at the 13
adults.

## 1. Changes that touch the confirmatory outcomes

**C1 (jaw motion is not a hinge)**

- D3: the in-vivo noise floor is the quietest 1-s window of rest. E28 later replaced this floor.
- E8: corrected noise model, in-vivo null P99 from 1.14 to 1.20 mm. The decision is unchanged (11 of 12 above the null).
- E19: bench null filtered like the data, P99 from 0.22 to 0.13 mm. The decision is unchanged (12 of 12).
- E28: in-vivo null from the participant's own rest residuals, P99 from 1.20 to 1.56 mm and ratio from 1.74 to 1.19. This made a confirmation harder (the null is conservative by construction), and the decision is unchanged (11 of 12).
- D17: cohort from 12 to 14, best-fit residual from 2.01 to 1.95 mm.
- D24 and D24 addendum: 1000 simulations, from 13 to 12 of 14 above the null, plus a cohort test (p = 0.0054). The added slow-noise and motion-noise variants do not support the claim (7 of 14 and 2 of 14) and are reported next to the primary.
- E32: the residual row includes every participant with optics, from 1.947 mm (n = 13) to 1.924 mm (n = 14).
- D33 and E38: post hoc test of the hinge through the condyle points against its own null (13 of 14 above). The primary is unchanged.
- D35 and D36: reported for 13 participants, 11 of 13 above the null (ratio 1.18).
- D20, D25, and J29: sensitivities and an exploratory extension. The primary is unchanged.
- A4, E2, E13, and D18: condyle-based outcomes are secondary. The primary outcome at the incisal point is unchanged.

**C2 (muscle activity precedes motion)**

- D5: closing leads are descriptive only, and the primary stays the opening lead.
- D6: a second onset rule is reported next to the pre-specified one. The effect does not survive it, so C2 is reported as not confirmed (a confirmation became harder).
- D8: new mains removal. The lead depends on onset selection, not on the mains fit.
- D14: masking change, first-onset lead from 99 to 162 ms and driving-burst lead from 34 to 56 ms (p from 0.14 to 0.046). We report this as the consequence of a post hoc change.
- D15: pooled channel-cycles, a 3-cycle minimum, and Holm over four tests. The first-onset rule stays significant in both tasks (open-close 108 ms, Holm p = 0.023 in both), the driving-burst rule does not (Holm p = 0.23 and 0.055), and 3 to 4 of 12 participants are excluded from the tests.
- E17/E26, E18, and E27: timing errata that barely move the leads.
- D23, D23a, and D34: a chance null next to the frozen test. The observed leads do not exceed it, and D34 reads the null as a window-bias quantification. The frozen test stays primary.
- D31 and E34: reporting against other onset sources, and disclosure that the latency chain overestimates the lead by about 5 to 8 ms in favour of "EMG first".
- D20, D29, C2 onset sensitivity (J3), X11 (IMU latency), and J17: sensitivities. The verdict "not confirmed" stays in each.
- E33: the minimum counts channel-cycles, not cycles (wording).
- D17 and E37: no change of the C2 values.
- X6: a post hoc family that does not change the C2 rows or verdict.

**C3a (wearable sensing recovers opening)**

- D7: repeated-measures limits of agreement, slightly wider.
- E9: 117 instead of 121 cycles in the agreement analysis.
- D17: cohort change, generic-model limit from 3.98 to 3.83 mm.
- D26 and D26 addendum: own model out of sample (2.87 to 3.05 mm) and a MOVER interval (upper bound of the generic model from 4.87 to 6.34 mm). Both make the estimate less optimistic.
- E32: generic model trained on every other participant with optics, limit from 3.83 to 3.92 mm.
- D35: reporting population, limit from 3.92 to 3.83 mm.
- D20: at higher low-pass cutoffs the limits widen (sensitivity).

**C3b (wearable sensing decodes intent)**

- A3: decoding inputs changed before any decoding outcome.
- E14 and D13: defect fixes and rest-relative EMG features. Unseen-subject accuracy of EMG plus IMU from 0.556 to 0.583 and of IMU alone from 0.620 to 0.607, so EMG plus IMU is no longer significantly below IMU alone.
- D4 and D32: the secondary within-subject cross-validation (D32: EMG-only from 0.612 to 0.528).
- E20: permutation test configuration, p unchanged.
- D16 and E37: no change of the decoding values.
- J22.4, J22.6, and J24: reporting statistics (participant-level kappa and recall, effect sizes). No test changes.

**Confirmatory SPM tests (section 5.2 of the plan)**

- E6: no change. D14: openers against closers with n from 11 to 12, masseter left against right still without a cluster. D15 and E25: the decision uses the Holm-adjusted p.
